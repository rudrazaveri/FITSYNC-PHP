Where & How is Data Stored?

100% Private, On-Device Browser Storage

FitSync stores all user profiles, custom workout logs, sets, hydration logs, and fitness assessments exclusively in your browser's private LocalStorage sandbox.

  • Zero Cloud Tracking: No remote servers, No Supabase, No Firebase, and zero telemetry sent to third parties.
  • Complete Offline Capability: The entire application operates completely offline without internet once loaded.
  • Instant Zero-Latency: Logging reps, updating sets, or filling water renders in under 1 millisecond.
  • Full User Control: You can wipe or reset the local database anytime by clicking "Reset Database (Start from Scratch)".
